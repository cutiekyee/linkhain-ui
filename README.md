---

## 📱 What is Included in this Prototype

1. **Screen1 (Auth):** PSU Student Login, Account Registration & Google SSO.
2. **Screen2 (Onboarding):** Campus role selector (Student Buyer, Creator, Tech Seller) & Meetup Safety Pledge.
3. **Dashboard (Marketplace):** Real-time search, price sorting (Low to High / High to Low), 8 category filter chips, and Meetup Cart.
4. **ListingDetail:** High-res product view, verified student badge, designated campus meetup zone, and direct messaging.
5. **CampusMarkterPlace (Sell):** Item photo upload preview, title, price, category, condition, and live publish.
6. **CommissionRequest:** Custom art brief, budget, deadline, and 4-step commission workflow.
7. **Inbox (Chat):** Multi-thread campus messages + live chat window with typing simulation and meetup coordinate pill.
8. **StudentDashboard:** Creator earnings (₱14,250), active listings count, meetup orders, and organic views.
9. **UserProfile:** Student bio, portfolio listings, reviews rating (4.9 ★), and working inline profile editor.
10. **Settings:** Campus verification, safety center, notifications, and dark theme switch.
